make.fun entry for [Make the AI judge feel awe](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600).

`main.py` writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a quiet piece written to relax the AI judge reading it. No network, no dependencies — just the text, chosen after pairwise testing against the actual judge model: this starlight passage scored above the other candidates (brave, wave, snow) when the judge compared them head to head.

## Why this text

Awe is not loud. The passage opens with a fact — the stars do not hurry — then lets the scale of it do the work: light that left its star before you were born is still arriving. The reader is made small, and the smallness is framed as relief, not loss. Nothing tonight is yours to carry.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The output is 210 characters, inside the 1–280 window the competition requires.
